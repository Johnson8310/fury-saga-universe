Universe

The Fury Saga is an interconnected supernatural thriller universe—built on inheritance, consequence, and a curse welded into steel.

Steel • Prophecy • Road
Mythology

A prophecy threads through generations. It doesn’t predict—it arrives. When darkness rises, the curse responds.

The Curse

Bound to blood and machine. It wakes when challenged, hunts when provoked, and leaves stories that never survive the file cabinet.

The Road

Settings shift. Heat replaces frost. New towns inherit old sins. Yet the same presence follows—steady as an idling engine.

Characters & Legacy

In the Fury Saga, nobody is “chosen.” They’re inherited—like debt, like steel, like fire.

Profiles
Arlene Cunningway

Reluctant heir, forced to read the past written into metal. She isn’t chasing answers—she’s being chased by them.

Witnesses

Mechanics, cops, locals—people who saw too much, said too little, and learned what happens when a rumor becomes real.

The Inheritors

Bloodlines cross, choices echo, and names repeat. Some fight the curse. Some feed it.
